03 / The story

The situation.
The complication.

Situation

In 2022 the estimated $6B Sprite business represented 78% of the Lemon-Lime soda category and nearly 10% of all sodas sold in the United States. Sierra Mist, itself a billion-dollar brand, pales in popularity and revenue, and as such didn’t offer consumers a true alternative to the lemon-lime behemoth.

Complication

PepsiCo, Sierra Mist’s parent company, had taken multiple stabs at a Lemon-Lime revolution. The internal memory of false starts & second-place finishes loomed large. And now it needed to coax Gen Z, the most sought-after demographic across a heavily saturated beverage market, to a new camp. But how? What needs to change?

Why me?

PepsiCo experience.
Generational fluency.

I knew PepsiCo and had taken big swings for there before.

Eight years driving marketing & innovation projects through the ecosystem & navigating the bureaucracy gave me the vocabulary, operations, & commercial know-how to push ideas forward. This comes in handy when bold new ideas need to trickle down into the organization. Specifically, the way we talked about Gen Z needed to be as palatable for the CMO as the finance manager.

I knew a bit about the next generation.

As a former grade-school teacher, high school basketball coach, record label associate, & marketer working on youth-forward brands, tutor, & teen mentor, I spent my entire career breathing the next generation. Time-on-task formally & informally researching young people granted me a facility with what to look for, how to ask questions, & how to synthesize what I learned. This informed my work & the larger strategy.

The steps

Decode the culture.
Reframe the brief.

  1. Desktop research & document review.

    Recent client work & past PepsiCo projects gave me loads of research on the consumer target & cultural climate. While freshly interrogating young people offered new data, I wasn’t starting from scratch. Revisiting past work allowed me to set & refine new hypotheses & explore provocations, saving both time & money.

  2. Host a preliminary Ways of Working session.

    I got a forensic view into how the strategy & creative teams collaborate on launch plan & asset development before deployment.

  3. Personally crawl & scrape target-rich digital platforms for new & corroborating insights.

    I took a deep dive into the forces & phenomena that drove a guttural sense of uncertainty for Gen Z. Despite softening pandemic lockdown restrictions, Gen Z had spent crucial time during their formative years in isolation. Worldviews shifted. A history that once promised the next generation better outcomes than their parents no longer could. Clues of malcontent & deeply tempered optimism for these digital natives littered the digitalscapes they purveyed, & thus their realities.

  4. Reframe the brief.

    From “Build a Lemon-Lime brand with the potential to capture 5+% of the market” to “Create the Insurgent Anti-Sprite product & brand that delivers the ultimate Refreshment from Reality.”

  5. Distill & draft.

    After iterating between 1:1 chats & online research, I drafted a primer that distilled their values, beliefs, priorities – and more importantly, how each shows up in their lives & what it all means in culture. Gen Z was malcontent because they were keenly aware of their vulnerabilities. But they want to fight. Awaiting their rallying muse, they brush back nihilism through meme & humor.

  6. Integrate overarching strategy.

    Workshops. Feedback sessions. Presentation iterations. All were necessary to help the team move from the abstract idea of a Sprite-fighter to convincing the CMO & commercial team for stage-gate approval to develop.

The results & outputs

From a secret project
to a national launch.

  • In September of 2021, the PBNA CMO & commercial stage-gate team approved official enterprise development which included new formula, pack design and visual identity, naming, & launch campaign development.
  • In 2022 the Lemon-Lime project officially became “Starry” & launched January 11, 2023.
  • By year end it achieved nearly $500MM in retail sales & earned ~5.3% market share of the Lemon-Lime category.
