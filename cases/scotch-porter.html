Summary

02 / The story

The situation.
The complication.

Situation

Stepping fresh out of a $11MM Series B funding round on route to an acquisition, Scotch Porter sported healthy revenue per DTC and brick-and-mortar retail footprint. Yet it lacked the unifying brand guardrails necessary to anchor its brand activities & commercial story. As it sought to expand its retail footprint it needed more cohesion. The brand’s year-over-year growth & fundraise drove the ambition to level-up its marketing foundation.

Complication

Scotch Porter, like the men it serves, was well on its dynamic journey towards “becoming”. Yet similarly, in the absence of a legible roadmap & resources (i.e. brand foundation), Scotch Porter let instinct fill the void. Ahead of the capital raise the brand, consumer target, & campaign efforts tracked rather close to Calvin’s story as the founder. Post-capital raise, it needed to appeal more broadly. But how do we develop an ownable brand position that moves beyond the founder to meet consumers where they are without watering down the richness of the origin story or losing its cultural tether? How do we bridge the past to the present while simultaneously projecting a future vision that’s attractive to shoppers & investors?

Why me?

The consumer.
The storyteller.

I am the consumer.

As an African American man with curly hair, I deeply understand the grievances curly-haired men have with grooming. Grooming is a bellwether for men on a broader self-care journey & I intimately know the pain points worth interrogating.

I know how to tell the marketing story.

I have experience preparing brands for acquisition or integration on both the buy and sell sides of a transaction. Whether it be PepsiCo’s ~ half-billion-dollar acquisition of PopCorners or piloting a boutique buy-side investment banking advisory to help mid-sized CPG companies tell better marketing stories to boost valuations & exit, I know what levers to pull to catch stakeholder’s attention. I have the insider’s & outsider’s view.

I understand how to integrate teams into the brand development chain. I’ve done it at scale.

From managing product innovation, brand repositioning, & launch campaigns soup to nuts at PepsiCo to consulting for startups looking to scale – I know how to quell egos, manage swim lanes, & unify commercial, creative, & agency teams into the iterative process of brand making.

The steps

Build the foundations.
Bring everyone with us.

  1. Craft the learning plan & collate initial hypotheses.

    With the team, I ‘handwrote’ the research plan & stimuli, & hosted regular early check-ins with the assigned innovation agency to ensure we synced timelines to share insights & progress. Our positioning hypotheses informed theirs, allowing a consistent thread through connected but disparate work streams.

  2. Reframe the brief.

    After stakeholder conversations, early consumer interviews, & informational one-on-ones with experts we refined the brief from “level up the brand positioning to anchor marketing activities” to “become the top-of-mind grooming brand for men seeking to define their version of masculinity on the precarious journey towards transformation.” The shift? Center the brief on a motivating consumer need, which will shoehorn the commercial ambition.

  3. Cull the insights into concrete strategy.

    The brand foundations needed to inform campaigns, communications, PR, partnerships, & product innovation. Working directly with the internal team & innovation agency running a parallel path on the product roadmap, I collated secondary research, interviews, & focus groups into positioning territories, consumer tribe persona options, & tone-of-voice guidance for copy across website, social media, and in-store assets.

  4. Codify into guidelines & guardrails.

    From the consumer tribe & muse personas, to the brand purpose statement & benefits, & an articulation of the tone of voice, I crafted & coordinated the creation of a digital playbook with single sheet “Brand Strategy on a Page” pullout that teams can hand over to creative, agency, & operations partners.

The work moved through discovery & background, stakeholder interviews, a hypotheses workshop, competitive analysis & conceptual mapping, field research, and deliverable development & innovation integration.

The results & outputs

A clearer position.
A stronger business.

  • Produced 1-pagers for adjacent businesses & identified the “true” Scotch Porter Competitive Set to establish the “white space” for differentiated positioning options.
  • Set Tribe, Design, & consumer target definitions, including brand “muses” to appeal to men who resonate with the struggle to “transform into their own definition of masculinity.”
  • Established guidelines for the Scotch Porter “personality” & tone-of-voice across physical & virtual channels including guidance for copy across website, social media, and in-store assets.
  • Drove 70% growth & 2022 IRI designation as fastest-growing male lifestyle brand in US Beard & Haircare & enabled national retail expansion in Walmart, Target, CVS.
  • Sparked “Fresh From The Kitchen” tagline & campaign, a precursor to expanded distribution and a full company sale in December 2025.
  • Informed New Product Innovation Roadmap & Refreshed Visual Identity System anchoring the brand in the journey towards transformation.
