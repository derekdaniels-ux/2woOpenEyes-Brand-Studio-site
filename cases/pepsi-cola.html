05 Brand Marketing Manager

Pepsi Cola
Product Reinvention.

Leading an expedited process to develop a next-generation mega-beverage worthy of the Pepsi namesake

Summary

After years of prodding by the PepsiCo Beverages Global President, CEO Indra Nooyi awakened to an epiphany; Project Verto (“the turn-around”) is born. The brief? Create a sub-30-calorie, all-natural, mega-beverage that can carry the heft of a Pepsi brand name & launch in time for the 2018 Superbowl. That is, reinvent the Pepsi beverage formula. I led a team of 42 cross-functionals & 10 agency partners to accelerate & complete product & brand development in 6 months.

05 / The story

The situation.
The complication.

Situation

By 2016 PepsiCo Beverages had ceded millions of cases globally to water and healthier alternatives. Over-night taxation on full-sugar drinks, changing consumer palates and preferences for health-forward substitutes, & the emergence of Better-for-You options presented headwinds as consumers right-sized their sugar consumption. For years the PepsiCo GBG President chirped to the CEO that “The name on the door is tied to a soda category in decline. We should do something.” When she decided on the moonshot I was asked to lead the work.

Complication

Many teams tried before to no avail. Pepsi Edge. Pepsi Next. Pepsi True. The institutional memory of these uphill battles was long. But consumers were voting with their wallets & begging for change. How do we galvanize an apathetic internal organization to try again?

Why me?

Enough to disrupt.
Enough to listen.

I knew enough to be “dangerous” but not ignorant.

As a relatively junior associate I had just enough knowledge about the PepsiCo system to strategically disrupt it, not enough to skip asking the right questions, just enough to question knee-jerk “no’s”, & not enough to become cynical that our moonshot was doomed from the start.

I telegraphed a core philosophy, work principles, & an innovation POV important for the unknown battles ahead.

“Strong Hypotheses… loosely Held.” “Yes…and” Attitude & Behaviors. “Safe To Try” Mentality vs Total Certainty. Entrepreneurial Agility Over “Comfort”. The belief in myself to back myself in hostile rooms is paramount.

The steps

Make the challenge
consumer-led.

  1. Gather my goods: internal stakeholder intelligence & team development.

    I conducted an informational tour of people on the inside to interrogate the beliefs and/or mythologies around the enterprise for why this project will or will not work. This knowledge helped me identify which potential landmines I didn’t want to kick down the road & helped me assess which teammates I needed to activate early to shape the process & deliverables for which they’d later champion as their own baby.

  2. Reframe the brief into a motivating consumer challenge.

    Ultimately the enterprise’s brief is to make more money. Flatly, to do so we need to build urgency & deep interest around solving a meaty consumer need. Instead of manufacturing a 30-calorie all-natural soda, our charge morphed to “create a Pepsi mega-beverage for a ‘post-sugar’ era for which we’ll proudly allow our loved ones to build a habit that lasts a lifetime.”

  3. Draft the research, learning, & status reporting plan.

    Because our multi-million-dollar budget came directly from then CEO Indra Nooyi’s “slush fund”, her gaze was inevitable. We knew, however, that we needed an appropriate level of distance to execute the messy & often circuitous upfront brand & product development process. The compromise?

    I shipped weekly progress reports through the global President. I collaborated with agencies & cross-functionals (R&D, Design, Manufacturing, etc.) to steer 8-week sprints with live CEO & PEP leadership briefings at the end of each period. No briefings held at the CEO’s “desk”. No boardroom tables. We held conversations in the environments where the work was being done. In the R&D lab. In a pop-up room fabricated by our design team. We made it experiential. We didn’t want Fortune’s 2017 2nd-Most Powerful Woman in the world in “critique mode”. We wanted her in “build mode”.

  4. Execute.

    I didn’t just lead from the front or use the mandate from the CEO to crack the whip. I got my hands dirty. I braved sub-freezing temperatures in Beijing to moderate ethnographies with coat-latent Chinese respondents & interpreters. I braved police questioning in the Mexico City airport when security suspected my armored case of handmade product samples was contraband – all to conduct taste tests & allay product concerns from a PepsiCo C-suite stakeholder. I partnered with internal & external teams after-hours to craft focus group stimuli & complete research, draft presentations, write test concepts, & more to create unanimity around our pursuit.

  5. Accelerated beverage, brand strategy, & campaign development.

    On a team in which I was the only person working full-time on the project we created a fully-formed brand & product, ready to present to the PepsiCo Executive Committee (PepsiCo’s Board of Directors) 180 days after the project start. Comparable projects take 2-4 years.

The results & outputs

A moonshot built
for the long game.

  • New GTM approach: secured recording artist & tech entrepreneur Will.I.Am of The Black Eyed Peas as the “Chief Creative Officer”. Secured verbal agreements with STXFilms (“Hustlers”, “Bad Moms”, “Molly’s Game”) to develop a creator-led communications campaign.
  • 200MM+ process & formula improvement value. New IP & manufacturing capabilities provided direct opportunities to upgrade sweetener technology & address production inefficiencies across the PEP system. Mid-to-low-calorie PEP drinks today leverage this system.
  • President’s Award & Executive Honors. The team received executive level & PepsiCo Executive Council (Board) recognition for responsible risk-taking, collaboration, & inspiration.
  • Through VP level & 10+ agencies, completed consumer research, produced proprietary packaging, formula, & sweetener system, secured 4 pack & process patents, & outlined top-line positioning & campaign directions.
