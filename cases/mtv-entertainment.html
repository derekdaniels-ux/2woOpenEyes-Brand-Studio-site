04 / The story

The situation.
The complication.

Situation

When MTV Entertainment executives tamped down on a proposal to reposition MTV Entertainment upon the entry of a new Paramount/ViacomCBS CMO, the Marketing Strategy SVP instead asked me to position 8 show & film debuts across its sibling channels. Having ceded 30MM+ “cord-cutting” cable subscribers to streaming platforms in the prior 2 decades, the network sought a fresh point of view as it launched programming with more mature topics for discovery-focused viewers.

Complication

Legacy viewers associate MTV Entertainment, and by extension its sibling channels, with 1990s & early aughts reality nostalgia. Modern viewers discover mature content through algorithmic streaming feeds. Binge-expectations in a content-saturated environment make episodic one-offs less appetizing. Show launch assets needed to “work harder” to draw eyeballs, & in this case propel vaunted but relatively unknown film acquisitions into Oscar-worthy contenders. But how?

Why me?

Know the audience.
Unlock the DNA.

I had experience positioning brands for MTV Entertainment’s target audiences.

From small startups to enterprise legacy brands, I’ve built brand strategy across the range & for each of the core psychographic & demographic profiles the network aimed to reach with new programming.

I know how to uncover, unlock, & transmute the most potent elements of a brand’s DNA to consumers.

From creating & attracting audiences to inaugural live cultural experiences for Fortune 50 companies to coordinating global launch content for upstart brands – all on shoestring budgets – I have experience pulling forward the crowd-pleasing essence of a brand offering & converting attention into participation.

The steps

Make every brief
work harder.

  1. Host a preliminary Ways of Working session.

    I got a forensic view into how the strategy & creative teams collaborate on launch plan & asset development before deployment.

  2. Reshape the (briefing) mold.

    Brand strategists relied on pro forma briefing templates, sometimes only briefly viewing the full program to execute the volume of briefs on order. This created churn for content creation teams down the chain who had to rewatch shows to confirm the imperatives laid out in the original document.

    From a 3/4th page brief with recycled sections from past templates to a multi-page document with time-stamped content references as “short cuts” for content producers to anchor.

  3. Align direction. Review assets. Iterate. Deploy.

    Everything from selecting which section of a film score to use in the trailer to prioritizing which award laurels to highlight on a social media static ad matter when the team needs to court viewers & award show voting members alike. I iterated with the team to align these choices to the content pillars, tone of voice, viewer psychology, creative filters, & content hooks I pulled forward in the brief. Together with media & social teams in the chain we solidified the specific launch plan needs (e.g. “For Your Consideration” ads for print/trade publications, trailers for long-form content on YouTube, chyrons for director & producer interviews, & still frame graphics for reviews, etc.).

    From relying on yesteryear’s viewer cohort data to streamlining bespoke desktop research sprints to hook the messaging hierarchy, content pillars, & key art direction in relevant current events & viewer psychologies.

Wrote & coordinated agency briefings/asset alignment to produce launch collateral including trailers, static/banner ads, trade publication collateral, etc.

The results & outputs

Eight launches.
Lasting recognition.

  • Show distribution across 12+ linear TV & streaming channels including MTV, Paramount+, History Channel, Smithsonian Channel, Prime Video, Hulu, YouTube, The Roku Channel, Philo, Apple TV+, Fubo, & Fandango.
  • Of the 8 show and film launches there were 40 awards nominations and 14 wins, including 3 Oscar and Emmy short-listed program.
  • The case-study presentation reports two Oscar-shortlisted films and a night few will forget.
